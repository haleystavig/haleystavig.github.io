About

Drawn to
the details.

I'm Haley — currently studying architecture and drawing houses on the side. My portraits translate the character of a building into line and form, each one drawn by hand with care.

Architecture school has sharpened how I see buildings — the way light hits a roofline, how a porch changes the relationship between a house and the street, what makes one home feel like someone actually lives there. I try to bring all of that into every portrait I draw.

Whether it's a family home passed down through generations or a first house you're proud of, I'd love to capture it for you.
